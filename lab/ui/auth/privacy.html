Account privacy

This page describes the data used by Google sign-in on agent-yes.

Google profile information

Google handles your Google account authentication. When you continue with Google, agent-yes receives your Google account identifier and basic profile information, including your name, email address and profile image. This information creates or links your agent-yes account and identifies your signed-in profile. Google sign-in does not give agent-yes your Google password.

Sessions and cookies

Authentication uses Better Auth and a Cloudflare-hosted database to store account and session records. Session cookies keep your browser signed in. Temporary OAuth verification information protects the sign-in flow. The authentication service also uses request and session information for security and rate limiting. OAuth tokens stored by the authentication service are encrypted.

Your agents are separate

Your website account is separate from the tokens and permissions used to connect to terminals and shared agent rooms. Signing in with Google does not itself grant access to an agent, change a terminal's permissions, or replace a room's access token.

Optional device sync

Check Sync beside a saved room to share its credentials, agent status and schedule times automatically with other open consoles signed in to the same account. New rooms start unchecked. Browsers exchange the selected data directly over encrypted WebRTC connections and save copies locally for offline use. Jobs keep running on their original hosts.

The account service verifies your session and relays connection setup messages. Room identifiers, credentials, host details and schedules are never written to the account database. Unchecking stops future sharing of that room when devices next connect. Signing out clears received copies and sync selections on this browser. Rooms you saved yourself stay available locally.

Signing out

Signing out ends this browser session. It does not delete your account records or revoke access in your Google Account. You can review connected services in your Google Account.
