YOUR ACCOUNT

Account terms

Google sign-in creates or connects an agent-yes website account using the Google account you choose. The same sign-in button works for new and existing accounts.

Using your account

Use a Google account you are authorized to use. Keep your Google account and browser sessions secure. The account menu shows the current signed-in identity and lets you sign out.

Access to agents

Website sign-in is separate from access to terminals and shared agent rooms. Connecting to or controlling an agent still requires that agent's existing access token and permissions. A website account does not give you permission to access another person's agents or data.

Google and account data

Google handles its sign-in service under Google's own terms. agent-yes uses the returned account identifier and basic profile information to maintain your website account and session, as described in Account privacy. agent-yes does not receive or store your Google password.

Signing out

Use the account menu to end your current browser session. Signing out does not delete your account, sign you out of Google, or automatically disconnect existing terminal and room connections.
